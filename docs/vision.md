# Apparel & Footwear LCA Intelligence Platform

Mission:

Enable one-click Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) for apparel and footwear products.

Traditional ESG systems require extensive supplier and process data.

Our system reconstructs manufacturing routes, provenance, machines, consumables, energy usage, transportation, and emissions using minimal user inputs.

Primary Inputs:

- BOM
- Tech Pack
- Product Description
- Product Images (future)

Primary Outputs:

- Carbon Footprint
- Water Footprint
- Chemical Inventory
- Provenance Reconstruction
- Process Breakdown
- Confidence Score

Core Differentiator:

Manufacturing Intelligence Engine
